Studying Abroad From Finland.

Moving from Finland to the United States to study and play basketball at Xavier University has been one of the most challenging and rewarding decisions of my life.

← Back to Home

Coming to Xavier

As an international student-athlete from Finland, I chose to continue my education and basketball career at Xavier University in Cincinnati, Ohio. Adjusting to a new country, culture, and academic system while competing at the collegiate level pushed me to grow in ways I never expected.

Cultural Adjustment

Adapting to a new country, language nuances, and everyday life far from home taught me flexibility, independence, and confidence in unfamiliar situations.

Academics in the U.S.

Navigating the American higher education system as a Business Management major has broadened my perspective on learning styles, classroom culture, and academic expectations.

Student-Athlete Life

Balancing Division I athletics with international student status added an extra layer of responsibility, from visa requirements to being far from my usual support system.

What This Experience Has Taught Me

Studying abroad has shown me how much growth happens outside of your comfort zone. Leaving Finland to build a new life at Xavier taught me adaptability, resilience, and how to find community in an unfamiliar place. It has given me a global perspective I carry into everything I do, on and off the court.

Looking Ahead

I'm grateful for the perspective this experience has given me, and I hope to use what I've learned navigating two cultures to bring a global mindset into my future career.
