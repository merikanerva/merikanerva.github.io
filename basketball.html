Playing at Xavier

I've had the privilege of playing collegiate basketball at Xavier University, where I currently serve as team captain. Balancing Division I athletics with a rigorous academic schedule has taught me time management, resilience, and how to perform under pressure.

Team Captain

Elected by my teammates and coaching staff to lead the team, set the tone in practice, and hold everyone accountable to our standards.

On-Court Growth

Every season has pushed me to sharpen my fundamentals, basketball IQ, and ability to perform in high-pressure, competitive moments.

Team Culture

Passionate about building a culture of trust and accountability, where teammates support one another through wins and losses alike.

What Basketball Has Taught Me

Being a student-athlete at Xavier has taught me that consistency beats intensity — showing up every day, in the classroom and in the gym, is what creates real growth. Leading a team has shown me how to communicate under pressure, hold others accountable with empathy, and stay resilient when things don't go as planned.

Looking Ahead

As I finish out my collegiate career, I'm focused on leaving the program better than I found it, mentoring teammates, and carrying the lessons I've learned as a captain into my future career.
