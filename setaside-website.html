The problem

The money lands. The tax part is on you.

NIL and school revenue-share payments are generally taxable. When nothing is taken out before the money reaches you, it's up to you to set the tax part aside.

No tax withheld

For self-employed athletes, the IRS says "there's typically no tax withheld" from NIL income, including school revenue-sharing payments.

Source: IRS, What is NIL income?
About 1 in 2

In an NCAA study from fall 2021, about half of athletes (49% of men, 50% of women) wanted more education on tax literacy.

Source: JMCO, on the NCAA well-being study

Taxes

Straight talk

What SetAside is not.

It's an estimate to help you plan, not a tax return. Here's exactly where it stops.

  • Not tax advice. It doesn't replace a tax professional, and it says so on screen.
  • Federal only, 2025 rules. It uses 2025 US federal figures for a single filer and leaves out state tax.
  • Doesn't file or move money. No bank connection, no payments to the IRS, no tax filing.
  • Tax math not yet checked. The self-employment tax steps haven't been checked by a tax professional yet.
  • Big money, real adviser. If you expect more than $1 million a year, get your own financial adviser.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is SetAside for?

College athletes who get NIL money, school revenue-share payments, or both, and want to know how much to set aside before spending.

How is the tax amount worked out?

SetAside estimates your federal tax for the year from your expected income, using the 2025 US federal tax brackets and self-employment tax. It then applies that share to this payment and subtracts any tax already taken out.

Tap "How was this figured?" in the app to see every step.

What if I don't know whether tax was taken out?

Choose "Not sure." SetAside assumes nothing was taken out, so the set-aside covers it, and suggests checking your pay stub or asking your athletic department.

What if I'm paid every month, or all at once?

Pick how the money is paid: one payment, every month, or your whole year's income at once. For monthly money, SetAside shows your split for each month and what it adds up to over 12 months. For a whole-year payment, it treats that amount as your income for the year.

Does it keep track of my income?

Yes, from the payments you save. My payments shows your income so far this year next to your yearly guess, split by payment type. If your saved payments add up to more than your guess, SetAside uses the higher amount for the tax estimate and tells you.

Where is my information stored?

Only in the browser on your own device. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data, your saved payments are gone.

Does it include state taxes?

Not in this version. It covers US federal tax only.

Is the 70% savings split a recommendation?

No. It's a starting point so you see a full split right away. Change it to whatever works for you.
