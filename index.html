About Me

Being a student-athlete has taught me how to balance competing priorities, remain accountable to others, and continue pursuing long-term goals even when progress comes slowly. Through academics and athletics, I have learned that consistent effort creates lasting results.

Education

Junior Business Management major at Xavier University and Dean's List honoree committed to academic and personal growth.

Leadership

As team captain, I help create a culture of accountability, resilience, and confidence while supporting teammates through challenges.

Professional Interests

Interested in athletics administration, universities, and the NCAA, with a focus on understanding how organizations operate behind the scenes.

What Motivates Me

One of the most rewarding aspects of leadership has been helping teammates who lacked confidence learn to view mistakes as opportunities for growth. People who know me best describe me as observant, supportive, and ambitious. I enjoy helping others achieve their goals while continuing to work toward my own.

Looking Ahead

I am seeking opportunities to learn how successful organizations are built and led. My goal is to combine the lessons I've gained through academics, athletics, and leadership to make a meaningful impact wherever I contribute.
